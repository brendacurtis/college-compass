A note on GPA: the federal College Scorecard dataset this site runs on doesn't publish admitted students' GPA, class rank, or course rigor — only standardized test scores and acceptance rates. So Reach/Match/Safety below is based on test scores only. Your GPA is shown for your own reference; weigh it (and course rigor) as important context the algorithm can't see.
