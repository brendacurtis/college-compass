Find My Schools

Enter your test score (and GPA, if you like) to browse every four-year college sorted into reach, high target, target, and safety schools. These are the same categories and estimates used on Build My List, which picks a balanced list for you and adds legacy and early-round options.

How to read this: each school's chance is a rough estimate from its acceptance rate, adjusted for where your score falls in its admitted-student range and for your GPA (if you enter one). Leave GPA blank and it's treated as average. College Scorecard has no GPA, rigor, essay, or activity data, so these are not predictions. Enter a home state to adjust a few public universities for in-state vs. out-of-state admission and cost. For-profit schools and the U.S. service academies are left out.
